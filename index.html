Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get started.

How do I place an order?

Choose your preferred package, then contact us through WhatsApp or submit the request form. We'll confirm your requirements and explain the next steps.

Can you create a CV if I don't already have one?

Yes. You can provide your education, work experience, skills and other relevant information, and we'll use the information you provide to create your CV.

Will my CV be ATS-friendly?

Our CV packages use a clean, structured format designed to be easier for applicant tracking systems to read. ATS performance can vary depending on the employer's specific system and job requirements.

What is the difference between the packages?

Basic includes a professional CV. Standard adds a professional cover letter, while Premium includes the CV, cover letter and LinkedIn optimization.

How long will my order take?

Delivery time depends on the service, the information provided and the current workload. We'll confirm the expected delivery time with you before work begins.

Can I contact you before choosing a package?

Absolutely. If you're unsure which package is right for you, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you determine the most suitable option.
